## About Me

I am interested in building **speech-native intelligent systems** that go beyond transcription.

My work explores how speech models can **understand spoken information, interact in real time, use tools, maintain memory, and improve through feedback**.

Currently, I focus on the intersection of:

`Speech LLM` · `ASR` · `Reinforcement Learning` · `Voice Agent` · `Retrieval` · `Memory`

---

## Research Interests

### 🎙️ Speech Language Models

Speech recognition, audio understanding, temporal grounding, and speech-language alignment.

### 🧠 Reinforcement Learning for Speech

Sequence-level optimization for ASR and Speech LLMs, especially objectives beyond token-level cross entropy such as WER, CER, retrieval accuracy, and task-level rewards.

### ⚡ Realtime Voice Agents

Full-duplex speech interaction, interruption handling, tool use, long-running tasks, and speech-to-speech agent systems.

### 🔎 Speech Retrieval & Memory

Contextual biasing, hotword retrieval, personalized memory, and retrieval over long-term spoken interactions.

---

## Currently Exploring

* **RL for high-accuracy ASR**
  How can reinforcement learning effectively optimize the small but important errors remaining in strong ASR models?

* **Speech-native memory**
  What information should a voice agent remember beyond text transcripts — speaker identity, prosody, pronunciation, acoustic context, or interaction history?

* **Retrieval-augmented speech recognition**
  How can external context, entities, and user-specific knowledge reliably improve recognition of rare or ambiguous words?

* **Realtime speech-to-speech agents**
  How should an agent perceive, reason, act, and communicate while sharing the same continuous time stream with a human?

---

## Research Questions I Care About

> **How do we move from “speech recognition” to intelligent systems that truly operate through speech?**

I am particularly interested in systems that can:

**Listen** → understand speech and acoustic context
**Retrieve** → connect speech with external and personalized knowledge
**Reason** → interpret intent and decide what to do
**Act** → use tools and execute long-running tasks
**Remember** → maintain useful information across interactions
**Learn** → improve from task-level feedback and reinforcement learning
